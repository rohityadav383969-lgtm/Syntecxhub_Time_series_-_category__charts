# 📊 Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes company sales data to understand **sales performance, profit distribution, and business trends** across different regions and products.  
The objective is to extract **meaningful insights** that can support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit
- Identify top-performing regions
- Identify top-selling and most profitable products
- Compare sales vs profit
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Generate actionable business insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python**
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook**

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains the following columns:
- `order_date` – Order date
- `region` – Sales region
- `product_name` – Product name
- `sales` – Sales amount
- `profit` – Profit amount
- `quantity` – Quantity sold

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. Import required libraries  
2. Load the dataset  
3. Data cleaning  
   - Handling missing values  
   - Removing duplicate records  
   - Cleaning column names  
4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
5. Sales and profit analysis  
6. Data visualization  
7. Business insights and conclusions  

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed
- Region-wise sales analysis  
- Region-wise profit analysis  
- Sales vs profit comparison  
- Top 5 products by sales  
- Top 5 products by profit  
- Monthly sales trend analysis  

---

## 📈 Visualizations
- Bar charts for region-wise and product-wise analysis  
- Line chart for monthly sales trends  
- Comparative bar charts for sales vs profit  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- West region contributes the highest sales and profit  
- Some regions have high sales but low profit, indicating cost inefficiencies  
- Certain products generate higher profit despite lower sales volume  
- Sales increase significantly during festive months  

---

## 📌 Conclusion
The analysis highlights **profitable regions and products**, identifies **areas requiring cost optimization**, and helps businesses make **data-driven decisions**.
